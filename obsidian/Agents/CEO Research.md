## EU Pesticide Regulation Trends

- **SUR withdrawn** 27 March 2024 (European Parliament rejected → no new hard law)
- **Farm to Fork targets** (non-legally binding): 50% reduction in pesticide use/risk by 2030
- **Progress** (July 2025): 58% decrease already achieved 2018–2023 — targets on track without new law
- **Immediate compliance driver:** PflSchG §67 electronic documentation (mandatory 2027 in Germany)
- PDF not permitted from 2027 — machine-readable formats (JSON, XML, CSV) required

**Business implication:** No regulatory shock, but steady documentation compliance demand = recurring SaaS need.
